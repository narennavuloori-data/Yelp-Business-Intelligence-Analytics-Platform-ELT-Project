# Key Insights

## Executive Overview

- The model contains approximately **150K businesses**, **7M reviews** and **2M unique reviewers**.
- Review activity rises substantially through the mid-to-late 2010s, reaches its strongest period near the end of the decade and then declines in the latest years shown.
- The dashboard sentiment score is approximately **0.89**.
- TextBlob classified **94.48%** of review text as positive.
- Five-star reviews are the largest rating group, at roughly **3.2M reviews**.

## Business Performance

- Starbucks is the most reviewed displayed business name, with approximately **21.6K reviews** and a rounded KPI value of **22K**.
- McDonald's follows with roughly **18.2K reviews**.
- Dunkin', Chipotle Mexican Grill and First Watch also appear among the highest-volume business names.
- The displayed average business rating is approximately **3.75**.
- The displayed five-star review share is approximately **46.23%**.

## Customer & Sentiment

- The average reviewer contributes approximately **3.52 reviews**.
- The dashboard shows about **385K negative reviews** and only **857 neutral reviews** under the TextBlob polarity rules.
- The average written review length is approximately **568 characters**.
- A small group of reviewers contributes more than one thousand reviews each.
- Positive review counts increase strongly from lower ratings to five-star ratings.

## Geographic & Category Insights

- The dashboard covers **27 states or regional codes** and approximately **1K cities**.
- Pennsylvania leads the displayed state review ranking at around **1.60M reviews**, followed by Florida at around **1.16M**.
- Philadelphia leads the displayed city business count at approximately **15K businesses**.
- Restaurants is the largest displayed category, followed by Food and Shopping.

## Interpretation Cautions

- Sentiment polarity is produced by TextBlob and is not manually validated.
- Grouping by business name combines multiple physical locations with the same name.
- Dashboard values are rounded and respond to slicer selections.
- Correlation between rating, review volume and sentiment should not be presented as causation.
